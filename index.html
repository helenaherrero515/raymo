The four stages of tab grief.

Stage 1 · 0 – 10 s
🥺 The Whimper

A soft, wobbly sine wave. Barely audible. Your tab just wants to know you still care.

"bakit mo ako iniwan?"
Stage 2 · 10 – 20 s
😠 The Side-Eye

Nasal triangle wave. Getting louder. It saw you open a new tab. It noticed.

"NAKA-SEEN LANG AKO??"
Stage 3 · 20 – 30 s
😡 The Confrontation

Harsh sawtooth wave. Your tab is now making a scene. Other tabs are watching.

"GRABE KA TALAGA"
Stage 4 · 30 s+
🤬 The Meltdown

Piercing square wave. Maximum volume. Your tab has given up on diplomacy.

"BLOCKED AND REPORTED"

How it works.

No servers. No accounts. No sound files. Just pure, synthesized disappointment.

⏱️

Idle detection

The service worker checks every 3 seconds. If a tab hasn't been active for the threshold (10 min, or 10 s in demo mode), it enters Stage 1.

😤

Favicon hijack

A content script replaces the favicon with an SVG emoji that escalates from 🥺 to 🤬. The tab title changes to a Taglish guilt-trip.

🔊

Synthetic screaming

An offscreen document runs Web Audio oscillators — one per tab. Pitch, volume, and harshness increase with each rage stage. Each tab sounds slightly different.

🤫

Instant forgiveness

Click any screaming tab and it goes silent instantly. Original title, original favicon, zero grudges held. Until next time.

Install in
30 seconds.

No store listing. No permissions popup novel. Just load it unpacked and watch your tabs lose it.

  1. Download or clone the extension folder
  2. Open chrome://extensions and enable Developer mode
  3. Click Load unpacked → select the folder
  4. Click the 😱 icon → check Demo mode (10 seconds)
  5. Open 5 tabs, click away, and wait for the screaming
